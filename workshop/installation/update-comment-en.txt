Island Airfields 2.0 is out.

Airfield gameplay now runs on the host through CC2X SDK. This update fixes landing cancellation, deferred queues and old requests crossing sessions. Your airfields are shown immediately in team colour, without flying over your own islands again.

Requires CC2X SDK 0.8.0 client beta 1:
https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3833014670

With the game closed, copy the SDK's dinput8.dll beside carrier_command.exe. Enable Island Airfields in Mods, then restart. Take an older manually installed Island Airfields DLL out of its loading location, if you installed one.

Screenshots and installation diagram:
https://github.com/Dioramawhois/-CC2X-island-airfields/blob/main/workshop/installation/README.md

Ordinary-host gameplay passed manual tests. A second remote client and dedicated servers remain unverified.
