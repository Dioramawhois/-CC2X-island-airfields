Island Airfields 2.1.2 - improved runway entry

Island Airfields is now a separate addon for stock CC2 and Revolution 1.6-19/1.6-20. Enable the original Revolution mod and Island Airfields together. No Revolution files or complete game screens are included. Carrier deck status excludes island landings.

Fixed-wing arrivals now join the outer runway approach circuit first. The owner confirmed the corrected oblique approach in an ordinary-host game. Native queued guidance beyond six kilometres and orders waiting for runway geometry to stream retain their existing behavior.

Requires CC2X SDK 0.8.1 client beta 1:
https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3833014670

With the game closed, copy the SDK's dinput8.dll beside carrier_command.exe. Enable Island Airfields in Mods, then restart. Take an older manually installed Island Airfields DLL out of its loading location, if you installed one.

Screenshots and installation diagram:
https://github.com/Dioramawhois/-CC2X-island-airfields/blob/main/workshop/installation/README.md
