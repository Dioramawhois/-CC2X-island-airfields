[h1]Island Airfields 2.2.1 - Parked Aircraft Display[/h1]
Aircraft fully parked at island airfields no longer clutter the vehicle-control map or active-unit lists. Access them through the airfield icon and its four hangar bays for graphical loadout changes and launch.

[list]
[*]Hide parked aircraft from map icons, pointer targeting and deployed-unit overview.
[*]Apply the same rule to Revolution aircraft sidebars, unit walls and box selection, including close map zoom.
[*]Remove the old parking ring and clear stale map selections after parking.
[*]Keep landing queues, approach and taxi visible. Departing aircraft return to the map when launch begins.
[*]Preserve four-bay management, graphical refitting, launch, flight policy and saved orders.
[/list]
[b]No new SDK DLL is required for this update if your installed SDK already supports aircraft refitting.[/b] The native holomap background is unchanged. Stock CC2 and Revolution 1.6-19/1.6-20 remain supported.
